# Horizontal Video

## Composition

- Default to 1920×1080, 16:9, and the source frame rate unless the destination requires otherwise.
- Give demonstrations most of the canvas; use camera picture-in-picture or side-by-side only when the face adds value.
- Preserve a consistent screen crop across adjacent actions so viewers retain spatial orientation.
- Remove private or irrelevant interface rather than shrinking useful proof to show the whole desktop.

## Pacing And Structure

Horizontal video can support a slower orientation, chapters, and deeper examples, but every section still needs a clear job. Use chapter landings and visual state changes to reset attention rather than adding filler transitions.

When the horizontal master is a source for vertical clips, record and edit with detachable beats and clean visual handles. Do not weaken the horizontal composition merely to pre-crop every moment.

## Captions And Graphics

Keep captions clear of player controls, lower thirds, code, and demonstrations. Use the destination's current overlay preview. Preserve sufficient side margins for television overscan only when the actual destination requires it.

Titles, diagrams, and code must remain readable at common laptop and mobile landscape sizes. Verify compressed detail after the platform transcode when possible.

## Review

Inspect chapter transitions, screen readability, camera inset placement, caption/proof collisions, high-motion sections, and playback on both desktop and mobile landscape.
