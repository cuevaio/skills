# Vertical Video

## Composition

- Default to 1080×1920, 9:16, 30 FPS unless the destination requires otherwise.
- Use face-only, proof-only, camera-over-proof, and designed graphic layouts intentionally.
- Fill every assigned region. Crop to cover; do not expose layout gaps or letterboxing.
- Keep face, hands, objects, and important proof clear of platform controls.
- Remove clocks, status bars, notifications, account names, and irrelevant interface before scaling.

## Pacing And Proof

Vertical viewing rewards a fast promise but still needs enough time to understand evidence. Use one central idea per short. Switch visuals when the audience's attention target changes, not on an arbitrary timer.

When screen content remains unreadable after a sensible crop, use proof-only framing, recreate the result as designed text, or record a closer source. Do not ask viewers to zoom.

## Captions

Meta does not publish a fixed organic-Reels caption position. Its conservative Reels-ad guidance recommends keeping the top 14%, bottom 35%, and 6% on each side free of important text. At 1080×1920 that corresponds to 269 px top, 672 px bottom, and 65 px sides.

For organic posts without a CTA overlay, a lower-third starting point near 320 px from the bottom can preserve more proof, but it must be tested in the current app and should not be described as officially safe. Apply the inset to the full caption box, including background and shadow.

Use short groups, no more than two lines, high contrast, and corrected punctuation and terminology.

## Review

Inspect widest captions over every layout, smallest proof at phone size, first/last transition frames, privacy crops, and a final playback with current platform controls simulated.
