# Repurpose Content

Repurposing is adaptation, not duplication. Every derivative needs its own complete audience job while remaining traceable to the canonical source.

## Creator defaults

For this creator, read [the saved creator style](../content-voice/references/creator-style.md). Each clip should carry a complete useful idea; accompanying copy can recommend the source and share a supported personal takeaway. Preserve the original speaker's ownership of anecdotes and results. Every request for finished clips, reels or shorts routes to [youtube-remotion-clips](../youtube-remotion-clips/SKILL.md) with the approved preset automatically, regardless of source. The creator does not need to request their usual style or mention a previous production. Select the ideas and source ranges, then continue through rendering the requested count; do not stop at the candidate ledger when finished videos were requested. Only an explicit style override changes the preset.

For Anthony's clip distribution, default to Instagram, LinkedIn, X, YouTube Shorts, Threads and TikTok, plus every available Buffer account in the selected organization. Carry destinations into the candidate ledger and handoff. Keep the same complete idea across destinations; adapt copy and packaging without choosing only the easiest platform. A current explicit subset overrides the default. Publication still follows the user's authorized scope.

## 1. Establish The Source Of Truth

Identify the canonical artifact, its brief, research pack, transcript or timeline, publication status, and `CONTENT_STYLE.md`. Record source timestamps, sections, or claim IDs so derivatives can be verified later.

Do not use an already-compressed social post as the factual source when the original research or master exists.

## 2. Find Atomic Ideas

Scan for segments that contain:

- a complete claim and proof
- a surprising but supportable observation
- a useful process or mistake
- a strong objection and answer
- a personal story with a clear implication
- a visual result that can stand alone

Reject excerpts that rely on missing context, overstate the source, or end before the landing.

## 3. Build The Candidate Ledger

```markdown
| Candidate | Source range | Complete idea | Proof | Audience job | Best format/platform | Adaptation needed | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
```

Rank candidates by usefulness and evidence, not by how easy they are to cut.

## 4. Adapt Natively

For each approved candidate:

- write a new opening that supplies missing context
- preserve the source's factual strength and caveats
- choose the proof that survives the new format
- change framing and pacing for the destination
- remove references such as “as I said earlier”
- create a native ending and CTA
- carry forward source links, caption spellings, and accessibility notes

A vertical clip may need designed proof rather than a crop of a dense horizontal screen. A written post may need the argument reconstructed rather than transcribed.

## 5. Protect The Canonical Piece

Name derivatives with their source ID and destination. Record content-level changes so later corrections can propagate. If the canonical claim changes, identify every affected derivative before publication.

The gate passes when every derivative stands alone, has a distinct audience job, preserves source meaning, links back to evidence, and is ready for `video-edit`, `social-post`, or `content-publish` without rediscovering context.
