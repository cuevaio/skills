# Record Video

Record **chunks**, not a perfect performance. A chunk is one complete thought or action with clean handles and a restorable start state.

Operate the recorder directly when possible. Otherwise, act as session producer: give one chunk and reset at a time, maintain the take log, and evaluate supplied playback evidence. A take is verified only by direct playback or explicit creator confirmation.

## 1. Open The Session

Require:

- content brief and script or talking points
- passed setup test and preflight
- current batch of beats
- visual or demonstration start/reset state
- continuity reference
- known privacy and factual risks

Prepare a take log with beat, take number, orientation, status, and edit notes. Confirm every required source is actively recording.

## 2. Rehearse One Chunk Ahead

1. Identify the one thought, emotion, or action to land.
2. Rehearse it aloud once or twice.
3. Set the demonstration, body, props, camera, and cursor to the documented start.
4. Confirm the intended eyeline and energy.

Open directly. Avoid repeating introductions, channel ceremony, or the outline unless the audience needs orientation.

## 3. Capture The Performance

- Say what matters before or as attention should move there.
- Narrate decisions and meaning, not every mechanical action.
- Keep cursor, hands, and props still when they are not communicating.
- Let personal reactions remain when they improve trust and do not obscure facts.
- Preserve extra clean room tone and stable frames for editing.
- For voice-over, record against the intended visual timing or marked beats.

When both orientations are planned, keep critical gestures, face, and objects in the center-safe region, but perform naturally rather than staring at crop guides.

## 4. Handle Mistakes By Size

| Mistake | Response |
| --- | --- |
| Small misspeak with a useful natural correction | Continue |
| Broken delivery, lost energy, or confusing visual state | Reset and retake the chunk |
| Uncertain fact, misleading result, or unsafe action | Stop, verify, repair, and retake |
| Missing source, clipping, focus, or sync failure | Preserve usable media, fix setup, and retake |

Finish complete sentences, pause, return to the resting state, and leave a few seconds of clean handles before stopping or restarting.

## 5. Verify Small Batches

After a few chunks, play back with headphones and at delivery size. Check source presence, sync, speech, focus, framing, proof readability, continuity, privacy, and factual correctness before recording a large batch.

The recording gate passes when every beat has a preferred verified take, the take log identifies alternates and repairs, and restorable demo/source state exists where pickups may be needed.
