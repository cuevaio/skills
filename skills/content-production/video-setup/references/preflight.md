# Video Recording Preflight

Adapt this checklist to the production rather than preserving irrelevant items.

## Content

- [ ] Brief and delivery asset are approved and available off-camera
- [ ] Claims, names, commands, and time-sensitive facts are verified
- [ ] Demonstrations, props, files, accounts, and reset states work
- [ ] Pronunciations and exact caption spellings are recorded

## Room And Creator

- [ ] Interruptions are controlled and the room is quiet
- [ ] Echo is acceptable
- [ ] Camera, microphone, lights, chair, and background match continuity markers
- [ ] Clothing and appearance match the intended continuity
- [ ] Water is nearby and voice/body are warmed up

## Frame And Privacy

- [ ] Vertical/horizontal plan and center-safe region are understood
- [ ] Captions and platform UI have reserved space where needed
- [ ] Notifications, private displays, mirrors, documents, and physical background are safe
- [ ] Passwords, keys, customer data, personal data, bookmarks, clocks, and status items are absent
- [ ] Screen zoom, font size, cursor, and display resolution are readable at delivery size

## Capture

- [ ] Correct microphone, camera, screen/region, and system audio are selected
- [ ] Levels are conservative and do not clip
- [ ] Focus, exposure, frame rate, and white balance are stable
- [ ] Separate source tracks are enabled when available
- [ ] Storage location, free space, power, and thermal limits are confirmed
- [ ] Record/stop controls and sync mark are known

## Proof

- [ ] A fresh representative test was recorded
- [ ] The test was played back with headphones at delivery size
- [ ] Every source is present, synchronized, readable, and private
