# Set Up Video Recording

Build the smallest setup that passes a playback test. Upgrade only to solve a problem the test exposes.

Operate capture tools directly when available. Otherwise, prepare one focused test for the creator and assess the returned clip, screenshots, metadata, or explicit playback observations. Keep the gate open until evidence passes.

## 1. Establish The Capture

Read the brief and script. Determine which sources are actually needed:

- microphone
- camera or phone camera
- screen
- system or application audio
- secondary camera or device
- practical footage or B-roll

Prefer simultaneous capture on separate tracks. If sources start separately, create a visible and audible sync mark. Decide whether camera, screen, or both carry the proof for each beat.

## 2. Choose The Framing Strategy

Decide before recording whether the canonical master is vertical, horizontal, or source-flexible.

- For vertical-first talking head, leave room for captions and interface controls without placing eyes too low.
- For horizontal-first teaching, reserve enough screen area for readable demonstrations.
- For both orientations, record camera and screen separately at sufficient resolution; keep the presenter near the center-safe region and avoid irreversible overlays.

Do not promise that one flattened frame can be cropped into every destination. Source separation creates the editing seam.

## 3. Fix Quality In Priority Order

1. **Speech:** close microphone placement, conservative levels, no clipping, hum, plosives, or desk vibration.
2. **Room:** reduce echo and interruptions.
3. **Proof:** make screen text, product details, objects, or demonstrations readable.
4. **Light:** create stable exposure and natural skin tone.
5. **Camera:** stabilize framing, focus, and white balance before considering upgrades.

## 4. Prepare And Configure

Load [the preflight checklist](references/preflight.md), remove irrelevant items, and add production-specific risks. Record exact device names, levels, resolution, frame rate, framing, zoom, light position, and screen settings when continuity matters.

Confirm each selected source by name. Keep microphone, camera, screen, and system audio on separate tracks when the recorder supports it.

At minimum, preflight must confirm approved delivery material, working demo/props, quiet room, safe background and screen, readable proof, correct named sources, conservative audio levels, stable exposure/focus, sufficient storage/power, known controls, and a fresh playback test.

## 5. Prove The Setup

Record 20–30 seconds containing normal and emphatic speech, representative movement, every required source, the final framing, and a sample of the smallest text or object viewers must understand.

Play it back with headphones at the likely delivery size. The setup gate passes only when speech is clean, every source is present and synchronized, proof is readable, framing is adaptable to planned formats, and no private or distracting material is exposed.
