# LinkedIn Adapter

## Shape

Write for professional context without corporate filler:

- use the opening lines to establish a specific tension, result, or lesson
- explain why it matters to the intended practitioner
- combine personal context with transferable insight
- use short paragraphs and descriptive bullets when they improve scanning
- land a clear implication before the CTA

Avoid résumé narration, inflated leadership language, fake humility, invented workplace stories, and engagement bait. A useful professional disagreement is stronger than a universal lesson with no evidence.

## Evidence And Voice

Name concrete constraints, decisions, examples, and outcomes. Distinguish what the creator observed from what applies generally. Preserve technical precision when the audience is technical rather than translating everything into business clichés.

## Links And Media

Make the post useful before the external link. Verify document, image, video, and link previews. Supply alt text and ensure cover text remains readable in the feed crop.

Do not make algorithm claims without current first-party evidence.

## Package Checks

- first visible lines communicate a real reason to continue
- paragraph breaks survive the composer
- tags name relevant people or organizations only with permission/context
- media and document order is correct
- CTA asks for a relevant action or conversation
