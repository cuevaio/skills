# TikTok adapter

Write a concise caption around the clip's actual idea. Supply missing context without transcribing the whole video. Use a few relevant lowercase hashtags when they help identify the topic. Avoid generic trend tags, invented first-person experiences and engagement bait.

Package the verified video, caption, first-frame cover preference, source attribution and applicable disclosure settings. Inspect current duration, media requirements, safe areas and caption truncation in the destination preview. Verify handles independently. With Buffer, check whether the account supports automatic video publication or needs a mobile notification. Record any manual action without claiming the video is live. The current Buffer TikTok metadata title is for photo posts; video captions belong in the post text.
