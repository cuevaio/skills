# Threads adapter

Lead with one useful conversational thought. Give enough source context to understand the attached clip and let the video carry its explanation. Keep paragraphs short, preserve actual speaker ownership and use a specific question only when it adds something.

Prepare a single video post by default. The Threads platform does not require an X-style multi-post thread. If a thread is needed, each entry advances the idea and the root remains useful on its own. Verify current text and media limits, preview, source links and platform-specific handles. Resolve handles independently; do not infer them from Instagram or X. Carry the verified Threads channel ID into the publishing package.
