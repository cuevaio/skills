# Script Spoken Content

Write for a mouth, an ear, and a visual timeline. Read `CONTENT_STYLE.md`, the content brief, and the research pack when available.

## Creator defaults

For this creator, read [the saved creator style](../content-voice/references/creator-style.md) alongside the local profile. Carry the plain, conversational language into spoken drafts, while treating spoken delivery as provisional rather than already calibrated. Use source context naturally and keep the speaker's experience attributed to them.

## 1. Choose The Delivery Asset

Use the least rigid mode that protects clarity:

| Mode | Best when | Produce |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bullets | The creator knows the subject and wants spontaneous language | Beat prompts, exact facts, transitions, and CTA |
| Hybrid | Hooks, claims, or transitions need precision but stories should stay natural | Exact lines plus bullet sections |
| Full script | Timing, regulation, sponsorship, translation, or confidence requires exact copy | Complete conversational script |

If an unscripted explanation already sounds like the creator, transcribe and structure it before writing from scratch.

## 2. Build The Spoken Arc

- Open with the audience's tension, a concrete result, or a specific observation.
- Establish why this creator is discussing it without a ceremonial introduction.
- Move one idea at a time and land each before the next.
- Put proof near the claim it supports.
- Voice the strongest reasonable objection.
- End after the takeaway and one natural CTA.

For short vertical video, earn attention immediately, keep one central idea, and let the visual carry information the voice does not need to repeat. For horizontal video, add orientation, prerequisite context, and chapter-level landings without padding.

## 3. Separate Speech From Production Notes

Use distinct fields for spoken words and visuals:

```markdown
| Beat | Spoken delivery | Visual/proof | On-screen text | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
```

Do not place camera directions inside teleprompter copy. Never write first-person experience, results, or opinions that the creator has not confirmed.

## 4. Make It Speakable

Read the draft aloud or use text-to-speech as a rough stress test. Fix:

- sentences that require a second breath
- noun-heavy or written-only phrasing
- repeated setup before the point
- transitions that explain the outline instead of advancing it
- generic creator language not supported by `CONTENT_STYLE.md`
- technical terms that need pronunciation or on-screen spelling

Estimate duration from an actual read when possible; word-count formulas are only a planning approximation.

## 5. Deliver

Return:

- three meaningfully different hook options
- the selected delivery asset
- a beat/visual table
- claims or personal details still awaiting confirmation
- pronunciation and caption spelling notes
- expected duration and how it was estimated

The scripting gate passes when the language is accurate, speakable, recognizable as the creator, aligned with visible proof, and complete enough to record without inventing missing transitions or claims in the session.
